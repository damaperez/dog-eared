Why I started this

Behind every well-loved plush is a story worth saving.

Some toys hold childhood memories, playtime adventures, or years of comfort. Whether they've been through natural disasters or adventurous play, I give each repair gentle, careful attention because I know how much they mean.

In a world where it's easier to replace than repair, I'm fighting back against disposability. I'd love to bring your plush back to life, so it can keep bringing fun, joy, and companionship for years to come.

"Love and care are the antidote."

— Demi, Founder
